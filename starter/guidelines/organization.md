# Organization and upkeep

Group notes by subject. Add nested directories when they make material easier to find. Keep supporting notes near the subject they explain.

These root guidelines apply throughout the vault. A scope can add its own `guidelines/`, read after the framework and root guidance. Nested scopes can add another layer.

Use `inbox/` for raw captures and thought bubbles. During inbox review, move useful material into existing notes or create structured notes for it. Remove abandoned ideas when the task allows it. Keep a short reason or original source when it would help later.

The owner decides when inbox reviews happen. Record a recurring schedule here if one becomes useful.

Keep attachments in the configured attachment location. The starter's Obsidian settings use `attachments/`.
